About me

Hello, I'm Israel. I lead design for brands and products.

I'm the creative director and lead designer at Rebrand — TheBrand™, a brand and digital agency, and I'm the brand and design director at SOMA, a school operating system now in beta. I started designing in 2017, with a background in IT and software engineering. Since then I've been creating scalable design solutions across different industries, and I enjoy collaborating with other people in the industry to bring them to life.
