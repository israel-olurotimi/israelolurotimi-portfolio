About me

Hello, I'm Israel. I design digital products and systems.

I design mobile and web products, from product architecture and user journeys to interface systems, prototypes and developer handoff. My work includes Lykluk, Reboot Monkey and Scout4Job. I lead product design for client work at Rebrand — TheBrand™ (which I founded), and I co-founded SOMA, an offline-first school operations product in beta. My background in IT and software engineering helps me collaborate closely with development teams. I also work across brand systems and creative direction.
